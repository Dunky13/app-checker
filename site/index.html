Public notice from Arie, canary

No humming on this Mac.

Kanarie is a free, open-source menu bar app for macOS. It lists the software on your Mac, tells you which of it sends telemetry, and shows you how to switch that off.

Noise complaint

To: whatever drones in the background
From: Arie, canary, in your menu bar
About: the hum

I do not sing. Not for seed, not for company, not for anyone. But a hum in the background I cannot stand.

So I keep an ear on this Mac. I go through your apps, Homebrew, the commands in ~/.local/bin, your global npm and pnpm packages and your editor extensions, and I give each one a colour.

I run in the background and start at login. When something new or updated may send telemetry, I send you a notification. Otherwise I keep my beak shut.

Kanarie itself sends no telemetry. Obviously.

Yours, crossly, Arie

Lost: one opt-out

Last seen in an issue tracker. Answers to the name of an environment variable.

Kanarie keeps those switches in one folder of findings, one per app, and checks them against your Mac.

His demands, colour-codedEvery item gets one of four colours. Arie has a mood for each.

Red

Stop that hum!

It sends telemetry, and there is no known way to switch it off. Arie sings at it, loudly.

Yellow

We want opt-outs!

It sends telemetry, and there is a switch. Arie ruffles his feathers and shows you where it is.

Green

Thank you for keeping it down.

No telemetry, or you switched it off on this Mac. Arie dozes.

Grey

Missingthe facts

Unknown. Arie cocks his head. Know the answer? Suggest a finding from the app.

He points. You decide.

Arie never flips a switch himself. When the switch is a command, you get one button, whichever fits:

  • Runruns the command once.
  • Add to ~/.zshrcputs the variable in the profile your login shell reads.
  • Copyfor anything else: you paste it wherever you like.

Run and Add ask first and show you the exact command or lines.

Kanarie changes nothing on its own.

House rules for a bird with a grievance

  1. No telemetry. Kanarie goes online to fetch the findings at launch and once a day (you can switch that off), to check for updates after asking you once, and for AI lookups and finding submissions when you ask.
  2. Open source, MIT licence. Every line and every finding is on GitHub.
  3. Findings by hand, with sources. One folder per app. A wrong one? Suggest a correction from the app. It opens a prefilled GitHub issue in your browser.
  4. Same rule for this page. It loads nothing from anywhere else.

Quiet wantedTake a tab. Free, for macOS 15 or later.

Install with Homebrew in two steps.

1. Tap brew tap dunky13/kanarie https://github.com/Dunky13/kanarie
2. Install brew install --cask dunky13/kanarie/kanarie

No Homebrew? Download the latest release, unzip it and move Kanarie to Applications. Then look for the bird in your menu bar.
