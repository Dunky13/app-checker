# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Mac users, many of them developers, who want to know which of their apps and tools send usage data, and how to switch that off. They usually arrive from a link, on a laptop in daylight, and decide in a few minutes whether this small free tool is honest enough to install. Some come back for the install commands or to look up what a colour means.

## Product Purpose

Kanarie is a free, open-source macOS menu bar app. It lists the software on the Mac (apps, Homebrew, commands in `~/.local/bin`, global npm and pnpm packages, editor extensions) and gives each item a verdict: red when it sends telemetry that cannot be switched off, yellow when it can be switched off (and how), green when there is none or it is off on this Mac, grey when unknown. It runs in the background, notifies about new or updated apps, and sends no telemetry itself. The homepage succeeds when a visitor understands the four colours, trusts the tool, and installs it.

## Brand Personality

Warm, honest, cheeky. The facts are exact and plainly said; the mischief comes from Arie, the canary who does not sing, except at something droning in the background. Dutch in spirit: direct, no fuss, says what it does and what it does not.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages: centered hero, gradients, three identical feature cards, "Get started" buttons.
- Privacy scare and hacker dark: black backgrounds, neon, padlocks, "they are watching you".
- Childish: Arie is charming, but Kanarie runs commands and edits shell profiles after confirmation, so it must stay trustworthy.
- Naming what inspired Arie: those who know, know.

## Design Principles

- Practise what it preaches: no third-party requests, no trackers, no web fonts from elsewhere.
- Facts first, Arie second: every claim comes from the README; the canary adds warmth, never vagueness.
- Colour means something: red, yellow, green and grey are the verdicts, and canary yellow is Arie.
- Show the real thing: screenshots and the real menu, not mock-ups of features that do not exist.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast in light and dark, verdicts never told by colour alone (symbol and word as well), visible focus, reduced motion respected, readable from 360 px wide.
